About FEIC 2019

The 1st Faculty of Engineering International Conference (FEIC 2019) was held at the University of Lagos, Akoka, Lagos, Nigeria from 15th–18th October 2019.

The conference brought together researchers, engineers, and industry practitioners from across Nigeria and internationally to present cutting-edge research on “Managing the Environment for Tomorrow.”

It was organised by the Faculty of Engineering, University of Lagos, under the chairmanship of Prof. P. C. Nwilo.
