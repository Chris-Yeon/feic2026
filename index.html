About FEIC 2026

The Faculty of Engineering International Conference (FEIC) 2026 is organised by the Faculty of Engineering, University of Lagos — one of Nigeria's most distinguished engineering faculties with a legacy of academic excellence and impactful innovation.

This premier hybrid event brings together researchers, academics, industry experts, and practitioners from across the globe to share cutting-edge research and best practices spanning all engineering disciplines.

All accepted proceedings will be published in Scopus-indexed conference proceedings, with selected high-quality papers invited for publication in the Journal of Engineering Research (JER) and other partner journals.

Conference Subthemes

Original research is invited across these nine engineering disciplines

Civil & Environmental

Smart, green and inclusive cities; climate-resilient infrastructure; AI for climate mitigation; renewable energy integration.

Mechanical & Aerospace

Robotics and human-centric automation; AI-driven manufacturing for circular and low-carbon industry; space systems.

Electrical & Computer

IoT and sensor networks; energy harvesting; wearables and nanotechnology; cyber-physical security; cloud, edge and distributed computing.

Chemical & Materials

Green process technology and sustainable materials for African industrial transformation.

Industrial & Systems

Circular economy and systems thinking; climate action; sustainable AI for resilient economies.

Geomatics

Positioning and geodesy; remote sensing and image analysis; LiDAR and point clouds; GIS; hydrographic applications.

Energy & Petroleum

Hydrogen, biofuels and geologic carbon storage; EOR, reservoir modelling and production optimisation; pipeline integrity and flow assurance.

Biomedical

Machine learning for early disease detection; wearable biosensors; ECG/EEG analysis; non-invasive BCIs.

Cross-Disciplines

Sustainable design, systems thinking and lifecycle optimisation; digital twins for infrastructure and Industry 5.0.

Submission Guidelines

Please read all requirements carefully before submitting your paper

Paper Requirements

  • Poster / Technical review / Work-in-progress — 250-word abstract
  • Manuscripts must be original and unpublished
  • Maximum 12 pages (template at jer.unilag.edu.ng/about/submissions#authorGuidelines)
  • Include: title, author names & affiliations, abstract (150–250 words), 4–6 keywords, corresponding author contact & email
  • Multi-author papers: indicate a contact author and one presenter
  • All submissions undergo rigorous double-blind peer review

Review & Publication

  • Accepted papers appear in the official Book of Abstracts
  • All accepted manuscripts published in Scopus-Based Proceedings
  • Selected papers invited for Journal of Engineering Research (JER) and other Scopus partner journals
